- Build an Unknowns Intelligence / Judgment Readiness layer between patient intake and autonomous or semi-autonomous action. Evidence: `README.md:L1-L11 @ main#f7219931b3dbe00e645ead2f54f00ebf6424185a (2026-04-28)`.
